DESIGN NOTES

A QUIETER ARCADE

The mix is deliberately low. A single continuous engine voice follows speed and throttle; event sounds are short and never stack.

Each machine has its own profile — high-revving bike, flat-six supercar, diesel haul, toy-bright duck and an electric chair whine.

Roadside ambience follows the scenery and the clock: city hum, country wind, desert static, mountain air or forest birds.
